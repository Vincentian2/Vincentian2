## 🧬 Research Interests

My research interests are centered on **Computational Biology, Molecular Parasitology, and Applied Virology**, with a particular focus on developing and applying computational solutions to infectious diseases.

I am especially interested in computational approaches for studying:

- **HIV**
- **SARS-CoV and SARS-CoV-2**
- **Ebola virus**
- **Marburg virus**
- **Dengue virus**
- **Zika virus**
- **Chikungunya virus**

## 💻 Research Focus

My work focuses on integrating **bioinformatics, structural biology, molecular modelling, cheminformatics, and in silico drug discovery** to:

- Identify potential antiviral drug targets
- Screen and prioritize therapeutic compounds
- Investigate drug repurposing opportunities
- Study viral genetic diversity and molecular evolution
- Evaluate protein–ligand interactions
- Explore mechanisms of antiviral activity and drug resistance
- Support the development of computational solutions for emerging and re-emerging viral diseases

## 🎯 Research Goal

My long-term goal is to contribute to the development of **accessible, computationally guided therapeutic solutions for infectious diseases**, particularly those that disproportionately affect populations in Africa and other resource-limited settings.
